# ReqGen Section 6 Registry Consolidation

Approved architecture: two workspaces only.

- `/registry` — Registry Centre with Overview, Incoming, Outgoing, Dispatch and All Operations tabs.
- `/registry/archive` — Registry Archive with retention, archive filtering, CSV export and authorised restore.

Legacy Registry routes remain as compatibility redirects into Registry Centre tabs.

Authoritative live data sources:
- `registry_correspondence`
- `departments`

Access:
- Admin
- Auditor
- Registry
- Registrar

Typography baseline: 13px.

Approved mockups are stored under `docs/approved-mockups/`.
